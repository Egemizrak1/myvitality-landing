01 — Recovery & health

Know when to push, and when to rest

Every morning MyVitality reads your HRV, resting heart rate and sleep, and turns them into one recovery score with a plain-language recommendation for the day. Your health trends live right underneath it.

  • Personalized recovery score built from your own baselines, not population averages
  • HRV, resting heart rate & sleep — with trends against your recent normal
  • Daily strain target that matches training to how recovered you are
  • Early-warning alerts when your training load climbs faster than your body adapts

02 — Sleep intelligence

See how you really slept

MyVitality builds a full picture of every night: a confidence-weighted sleep score, your stages through the night, and how tonight compares to what your body actually needs.

  • Sleep score from duration, efficiency, timing and restfulness — honest about limited data
  • REM, Deep, Light & Awake on an interactive stage timeline
  • Sleep timing — in bed, asleep, wake and sleep latency, detected from your night
  • Personal sleep need & sleep debt that adapt to you and decay as you catch up
  • Consistency tracking — how steady your bed and wake times really are

03 — Activity & strain

Every workout, properly analyzed

Track with GPS or sync from your watch — either way, MyVitality analyzes each session with heart-rate zones personalized to you and a strain score on the same scale as your day, so training and recovery finally speak the same language.

  • Personal heart-rate zones computed from your resting and max heart rate — editable if you know better
  • Activity Strain with a full heart-rate timeline and time-in-zone breakdown
  • Automatic workout detection — forgot to record? MyVitality spots runs, walks and rides from your wearable data and asks to save them
  • Live zones while you train — real-time zone and strain in the GPS tracker
  • Honest insights — pace vs your recent average, how this session compares to your typical

Discover routes around you

Open the map and MyVitality surfaces real runnable and rideable routes wherever you are — ranked for you, drawn on beautiful maps, ready to follow.

  • For You, Popular & Nearby route feeds for running, walking and cycling
  • Route generation — pick a style and distance, get a route built from real paths, parks and waterfronts

05 — Nutrition

Track meals in seconds

Photograph your plate or scan a barcode. MyVitality recognizes the food, sizes the portions and does the math from verified nutrition data — you just confirm and go.

  • Scan a meal — AI recognizes the foods and portions, nutrition comes from a verified food database
  • Scan a barcode — packaged foods pull real product nutrition data
  • Calories, protein, carbs & fat against your own goals, plus water and micronutrients
  • Net calories — what you eat minus what you burn, in one number
  • Everything editable — estimates are a starting point, you stay in control

Ask anything. Before it answers, the coach looks at your actual recovery, sleep, training load, nutrition and goals — then explains what they mean in plain language.

  • "How did yesterday's run affect me?" — connects strain, resting heart rate and readiness
  • Race predictions & taper guidance for your next event, from your real training
  • One intelligence layer across sleep, recovery, training and nutrition

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

How much does MyVitality cost?

MyVitality is free to start with a trial — no payment to join the beta waitlist. At launch it's one simple subscription (planned at $9.99/month, shown in your local currency), with no ads. The trial is risk-free — cancel anytime before it ends and you won't be charged. Beta members get early-access pricing. See the pricing page for details.

Does it work on iPhone and Android?

MyVitality launches first on iPhone and Apple Watch. An Android version is on the roadmap — let us know if you'd like to be notified.

Can I track workouts manually?

Yes. You can record a GPS workout in real time or add one manually with distance, duration and activity type. Learn more on the workout tracker page.

Does it connect to Apple Health?

Yes. MyVitality reads heart rate, HRV, sleep, steps and more from Apple Health, so any device that syncs to Apple Health works automatically.

Can I use it without a wearable?

Yes. You can track GPS workouts with just your iPhone and log nutrition and weight manually. A smartwatch adds recovery, HRV and sleep data.

What fitness metrics should I track?

Start simple: workouts, sleep and a weekly check on recovery. Our blog covers the best fitness metrics to track every week.

Is my health data private?

Yes. Your health data is never sold, never shared with advertisers and never used for marketing. Read the full privacy policy.

How is this different from Strava, Whoop or MyFitnessPal?

Strava tracks workouts, Whoop tracks recovery and MyFitnessPal tracks food. MyVitality combines GPS workouts, recovery and HRV, sleep, nutrition, an AI coach and community in one app — usually for less than any single one of them. See the full comparison.
