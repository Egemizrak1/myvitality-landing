Community Guidelines

Effective date: March 20, 2026. By using MyVitality, you agree to these terms. Please read them carefully.

What MyVitality is for

MyVitality is a community for fitness, sport, training, nutrition, routes and wellbeing. Share your runs, rides, workouts, progress photos, meals and routes — that's exactly what this place is for.

Always welcome

  • Training and race photos — including shirtless training, sports bras, swimwear in sport context
  • Fitness transformation and progress photos (non-explicit)
  • Bodybuilding, combat sports, yoga, swimming, hiking — every sport
  • Food, nutrition and healthy-lifestyle content
  • Routes, race reports and everyday context ("finished my run, grabbed a coffee")

Never allowed

  • Pornographic or sexually explicit content of any kind
  • Sexual solicitation or adult-content promotion (including subscription/DM offers)
  • Any sexual content involving minors — enforced with zero tolerance
  • Graphic gore, severe injury or surgical imagery
  • Harassment, hate, credible threats or encouraging harm
  • Spam, scams and malicious links

How moderation works

  • Every post is checked before it becomes public — automatically, in seconds.
  • If something is uncertain, it's held for human review instead of being published or punished.
  • If your post is declined and you believe it's a mistake, use Request review — false positives are overturned and help us improve.
  • Repeated or serious violations can lead to temporary posting restrictions or account suspension.
  • Public photos are stripped of hidden location (GPS) metadata for your privacy.

Reporting

Every post, comment and profile has a Report option (··· menu). Reports go to human review. You can also block or mute anyone at any time.
